LesserEvil Himalayan Pink Salt Popcorn

A simple snack made with a short list of ingredients

What's inside?

This popcorn combines organic popcorn, coconut oil, and Himalayan pink salt. The simple ingredient combination creates a light and crunchy snack with a subtle salty flavor.

Key Ingredients

Coconut Oil

Coconut oil is used to coat the popcorn and contributes to its light texture and subtle flavor.

Himalayan Pink Salt

Himalayan pink salt provides the salty seasoning that gives this popcorn its characteristic flavor.

LIGHT   ·   CRUNCHY   ·   SALTY
The result is a simple popcorn with a crisp texture and lightly salted taste, rather than a heavily flavored snack.
